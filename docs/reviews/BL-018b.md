## Invariants to review

- No partner/risk call inside a DB transaction. Durable UNKNOWN is committed before the only permitted submit.
- No timeout/404/epoch change/stale/conflicting evidence can cancel or expire unknown funds; no blind resubmission.
- Stable balance/SSE readers and volatile after-lock writer guard share holds+external gross reservation semantics.
- Account/policy/quote are checked before dispatch; current financial checks remain authoritative at settlement.
- Temporary own-reservation release, principal/fee/usage/outbox/audit/receipt commit once under bank locks.
- Failed settlement rolls back before a new transaction stores blocked evidence; later identical success may retry.
- SQL rejects mutable identity, incomplete commands, fake successful journals and forged/mutated history.
- Optional partner epoch precondition is backwards-compatible for old demo clients; core always pins it.
