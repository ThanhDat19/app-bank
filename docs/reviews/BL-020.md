## Review invariants

- OPERATOR-only scrape, bounded traces and cached/read-only snapshot. Public health minimal; sensitive actuators denied.
- Explicit metric allowlist and bounded method/template/status-class/outcome schema; no URL/query/identity/key/body labels.
- Manual OpenTelemetry local roots, no caller parent/baggage, selected four attributes, bounded volatile trace retention.
  No remote exporter, global SDK or tracing bridge. MDC/context restored and async completion records once.
- Best-effort telemetry failure does not replace business response. Sampling0 keeps metrics and generated trace IDs.
- Scrape/trace/cache GET do no SQL. One refresh at a time, read-only transaction and statement timeout; failed reads
  retain old counts/time with unavailable/stale flags. No false healthy zero or financial/schema writes.
- Benchmark owns synthetic JWT/app/PostgreSQL, has no arbitrary target/token/account inputs, fixed bounded workloads,
  no CI speed thresholds and exact financial closure. Owner chooses representative workload/SLO after baseline.
